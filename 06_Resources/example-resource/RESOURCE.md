A worked example: a short reference on the **PARA method**. Replace with a real resource of your own.

## Summary

PARA organizes information by actionability into four buckets: **Projects** (goals with
deadlines), **Areas** (ongoing responsibilities), **Resources** (topics of interest), and
**Archives** (inactive items). This vault's top-level folders follow PARA.

## Key Points

- Sort by how actionable something is, not by subject.
- Projects have a finish line; Areas don't.
- When a project finishes, move it to Archives.
