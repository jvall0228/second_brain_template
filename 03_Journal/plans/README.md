# Plans

Strategies, plans, and structured approaches for specific life areas. Unlike ideas (speculative) or insights (reflective), plans are actionable and prescriptive.

## What belongs here

- Financial strategies
- Health and fitness plans
- Productivity workflows and systems
- Any structured approach to a recurring challenge

## Naming

Use kebab-case: `my-plan-title.md`
