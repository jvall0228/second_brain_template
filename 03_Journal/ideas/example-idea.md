## The Idea

A small CLI that reads this vault's frontmatter and prints everything tagged
`status/active` across Projects and Areas — a one-command "what am I working on?".

## Why It's Interesting

- Turns the vault into a queryable dashboard without leaving the terminal.
- Reuses the tagging conventions already in place.
