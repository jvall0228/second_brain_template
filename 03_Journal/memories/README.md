# Memories

Personal accounts of specific events. Unlike insights (reflective observations) or daily logs (routine capture), memories are narrative recollections of notable moments worth preserving.

## What belongs here

- Accounts of trips, celebrations, milestones
- Stories of notable events or turning points
- Experiences you want to remember vividly

## Naming

Use kebab-case: `my-memory-title.md`
