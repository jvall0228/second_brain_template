# People

Notes and observations about people — their personality, strengths, and what you want to learn from them. These are character studies, not event logs.

## What belongs here

- Personality profiles and observations
- Strengths and traits you admire or want to learn from
- Relationship reflections

## Naming

Use kebab-case, typically the person's first name: `alex.md`, `sam.md`
